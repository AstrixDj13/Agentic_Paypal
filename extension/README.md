# PayPal Pocket: shopping memory (browser extension)

Lets your Pocket agents learn what you shop for outside PayPal, with your permission.

**What it shares:** product pages (pages that publish product data: name, brand, price, photo), how long you looked at them, and searches on shopping and search sites.
**What it never shares:** any other page, anything in incognito, and banking, health, email, document or government sites (blocked in the extension and again on the server).

You can pause it from the extension or from **What Pocket knows** in the app, delete any item, or delete everything.

## Install (Chrome, Edge, Brave)

1. Open `chrome://extensions` (or `edge://extensions`).
2. Turn on **Developer mode**.
3. Click **Load unpacked** and choose this `extension` folder.
4. In Pocket, open **What Pocket knows → Connect a browser** to get a 6-digit code.
5. Click the Pocket icon in the toolbar, check the Pocket address (`http://localhost:8787` locally, or your Render URL), enter the code, and click **Connect**.

Browse a few products on any shop. They appear under **Recently noticed** in the extension, and as interests and suggestions in Pocket.

## On Android

Chrome on Android doesn't support extensions. Install Pocket to your Home Screen instead. Then, on any product in any app or browser, tap **Share → Pocket**.
